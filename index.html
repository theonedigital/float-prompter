  1. Paste script → Build teleprompter.
  2. Tap Float it. A small window appears.
  3. Swipe home and open the Camera app (Video, front camera).
  4. Pinch the floating window to enlarge it, drag it to the top edge near the lens.
  5. Tap ▶ in the window, then start recording. The countdown gives you time.
The floating window is not captured in your recording — iOS only records the camera feed.
